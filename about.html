    Who I am and what I care about

    I am a front-end developer and designer with a passion for crafting digital experiences that feel intentional and refined. My work sits at the intersection of design and code — where typography, motion, and layout come together to tell a story.

    With experience across branding, UI design, and modern web development, I help clients and teams bring their vision to life with clarity and care. I believe great design is invisible — it guides without distracting.

    When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new typefaces, studying motion design, or seeking inspiration in architecture and print design.
